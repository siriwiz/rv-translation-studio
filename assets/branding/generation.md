# Logo generation prompts

Created with the built-in **imagegen** tool. The generated PNG exports are included in this directory.

## White on black

Single premium app icon with the exact letters **RV**: a bold bespoke white typographic monogram centered inside a black rounded-square tile, with transparent outer corners. Confident geometric strokes, a rounded R bowl, a clean diagonal leg, and a wide sharp V. Subtle forward-leaning cuts, disciplined negative space, and legibility at small sizes. Flat vector-like graphic; no slash, extra words, symbols, chrome, glow, perspective, or watermark.

## Black on white

Edit the first logo into its inverted counterpart. Preserve the monogram geometry, spacing, position, corner radius, margins, and composition. Use a white tile, black RV glyphs, a subtle neutral gray perimeter, and transparent outer corners. A final imagegen pass requested flatter colors and cleaner perimeter edges without changing the identity.
