RV TRANSLATION STUDIO uses Argos Translate 1.11.0 tokenizer components (MIT/CC0), CTranslate2 4.8.3 (MIT), SentencePiece 0.2.2 (Apache-2.0), and Sacremoses 0.1.1 (MIT). Model payloads are official Argos packages derived from OPUS-MT. Attribution and model-specific terms are retained in models/*/README.md. CC-BY 4.0 model attribution credits Joerg Tiedemann and Santhosh Thottingal, OPUS-MT — Building open translation services for the World, EAMT 2020, Lisbon. Studio excludes sentence splitter assets and runs packaged models with CPU int8 computation. Source: https://github.com/argosopentech/argos-translate ; model index: https://github.com/argosopentech/argospm-index .
Offline sentence boundaries use pySBD 0.3.4 (MIT): https://github.com/nipunsadvilkar/pySBD . Text identification uses Lingua 2.1.1 (Apache-2.0).
